What this tracker was built for

I built this tracker to give the branch director and headquarters a clear, monthly view of where the branch stood financially and how far enrollment needed to grow to reach profitability. It was submitted as part of regular branch reporting.

The break-even analysis was the most operationally useful part. By computing the blended revenue per student across all active course types and cross-referencing it against fixed and variable costs, I could translate enrollment numbers into a concrete financial target — 70 students to break even, 46 still needed at the time of this snapshot. That figure became the monthly enrollment goal communicated to headquarters.

I also tracked program end dates per course type to flag upcoming revenue drop-offs before they happened. Short-term cohorts ending within 1–2 months needed active replacement enrollment or monthly receivables would fall, which I flagged in advance rather than waiting for the drop to show up in the numbers.
